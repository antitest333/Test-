Our Craft & Philosophy

Born from a obsession with the perfect flake.

Flakes began in 2021 with a simple promise: to eliminate shortcuts from baking. We source organic heritage wheat milled in small batches, combine it with cultured Normandy butter, and let natural starters ferment slowly over three days.

The result is a delicate pastry shell that crackles upon the first bite, revealing a rich, buttery, feather-light interior that stays true to traditional French viennoiserie roots.
